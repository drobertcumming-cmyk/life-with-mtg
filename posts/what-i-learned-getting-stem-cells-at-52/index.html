What I Learned Getting Stem Cells at 52

By Marjorie Taylor Greene

May 29, 2026 · 3 min read

I did something a lot of people had opinions about: I traveled to Mexico and got a stem cell treatment. Since I've been open about it, I've learned there are a whole lot of armchair experts out there. So let me just tell you my own experience, honestly, and you can make of it what you will.

First, what these actually are, because there's a lot of confusion. The stem cells I received come from the umbilical cord and placenta of babies who were safely delivered. They do not come from aborted fetuses. I would never do that — I'm proudly pro-life, and my record backs that up. These are cells that too often just get thrown away, and they can do remarkable things for healing and regeneration.

Why did I do it? Because I take my health seriously and I'm always looking at how to age well and stay strong. This is a regenerative treatment — it works at the cellular level, and it's the kind of thing I'd rather invest in than wait around and treat problems after they start. It's also a treatment you essentially cannot get in the United States, because our FDA doesn't approve it. So I went where I could.

Now let me be careful and fair, because you deserve honesty and not a sales pitch. I'm not a doctor. I can't promise you a single result, and I'd be lying if I said there's no risk in anything like this — you should always do your homework on any clinic and any procedure. I did mine. The clinic I chose was clean, professional, and highly regarded, with its own lab on site.

What I can tell you is how I felt afterward. Within a few weeks I noticed my energy was better. I was sleeping better. I had more mental clarity, and a real drop in the inflammation I'd been carrying. That's my honest, personal experience — not a guarantee for you.

And here's something I want to say plainly, as a Christian. I don't think any treatment is going to make me live one day longer than God has numbered for me. I believe He's already set my days. But I do believe I can take good care of the body He gave me and improve the quality of the days I've been given. Those two things aren't in conflict.

If you're curious, do your own research — real research, not comment-section noise. Talk to people who've actually done it. Then decide what's right for you. That's all I'd ever ask.
