What Is America First, Really?

By Marjorie Taylor Greene

Jun 24, 2026 · 3 min read

People throw the phrase around like they invented it, and half of them couldn't tell you what it means. So let me tell you what it means to me, plainly, so there's no confusion.

America First means your family before Davos. Your town before a foreign capital. Your paycheck before a donor's portfolio. It's not complicated, and it was never supposed to be.

America First just means it's finally their turn. That's the whole idea. And it's worth fighting for.

It means we don't send your money overseas while your own people go without. It means we don't fight other countries' wars with American lives and American dollars because it's good for someone's business. It means the government you pay for works for you first — not the lobbyists, not the "international community," not whoever wrote the biggest check.

For most of my life I watched both parties operate on exactly the opposite principle. America Last, across the board. Endless foreign wars. Endless foreign aid. Endless money for everyone except the working families footing the bill. They dressed it up in noble language, but strip the language away and it always came down to the same thing: your interests came last.

America First is the correction. It's not isolationism, no matter what they call it. I want America strong, respected, and prosperous. But strength starts at home. A country that can't take care of its own farmers, workers, retirees, and sick has no business lecturing the rest of the world about anything.

And here's the part they hate: America First puts the burden of proof on Washington. Every dollar spent, every deal signed, every war started has to answer one question — does this serve the American people? If it doesn't, it doesn't happen. That simple test would gut half of what this town does, which is exactly why the people who profit from the current arrangement fight it so hard.

I believe in this country. I believe in the people who built it and keep it running — the ones who go to work, raise their kids, pay their taxes, and ask for almost nothing except a government that's on their side.

America First just means it's finally their turn. That's the whole idea. And it's worth fighting for.
